Instant Vlog Clip

A mobile-first web app that turns a pile of phone clips into a short narrated vlog: add clips, write a few lines or let AI write them, pick a voice or record your own, and get a vertical video with captions, a title and music.

The brief

Design the app around one feeling: “that was too easy.”

The app works end to end today, but every screen uses a plain placeholder look built only to prove the flow. Your job is to give it a real visual identity and polish every screen and state listed below. Layout and flow can change where you see a better way; the steps and the information on each screen are what the product needs.

Who uses it. Casual creators who film a lot on their phones and never edit: travellers, students, small business owners, “day in my life” posters. They post to TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. They are not editors and should never see a timeline.

What makes it different. The video is cut to a short first-person story (the script), read aloud by an AI voice or by the user. That’s what separates it from “random highlights” apps.

The client will send name, logo and brand colours later.

Design a system where the brand can drop in: keep the brand colour, logo and wordmark in a few clearly named tokens and slots. “Instant Vlog Clip” is the working name.

Principles to design by

  • Instant first, perfect second. The first result arrives with no decisions. Every option has a good default and can be skipped.
  • The video is the hero. On the editor, the preview is the biggest thing on screen; everything else supports it.
  • Edit by tapping and talking, not timelines. Changes happen on scene cards and by typing a request to the AI.
  • “Your videos stay on this device.” Users’ footage is never uploaded. This is a real promise worth showing where it reassures (home, export, sign-in).
  • Phone first. Every screen must work one-handed at 390px wide. Desktop is a centred column today and can stay that way.
What we need from you

Deliverables

  • Visual direction: one or two options for type, colour and overall feel, before detailed screens.
  • All screens and states in the inventory, at 390×844 (phone). Desktop for home, script and editor at 1280 wide.
  • Light and dark versions. The app follows the phone’s setting.
  • Component sheet: buttons, chips, inputs, cards, bottom sheet, progress, toasts, step bar, scene card, clip tile (list in Components).
  • Design tokens mapped to the existing token names, or a new list we can swap in.
  • Caption and title styles for the video: 4–6 presets with exact specs (see The video itself). These are burned into users’ exported videos, so they matter as much as the app UI.
  • Motion notes for the few moments that deserve it: generating progress, scene change, export complete.
  • App icon and favicon placeholders (final once branding arrives).

Figma is ideal. Name frames by the screen and state names used here (for example “Voice / Record / Teleprompter recording”) so we can match them to code.

Flow

Five steps, always visible

Each vlog moves through five steps shown as a step bar at the top. Users can go back to any step they’ve reached. A typical run takes about three minutes.

Home→ 1 Clips→ 2 Script→ 3 Voice→ 4 Make→ 5 Edit→ Export & share

Shortcut: on Script, “No script” skips Voice and makes a music-only montage. Sign-in appears only when a guest has used their free vlog.

Home

/p/:id/clips · step 1

Clips

Pick videos from the phone’s gallery (or drag them in on desktop). Each clip is read on the device; thumbnails appear one by one.

Content

  • Heading + one line: “Messy is fine”
  • Big “Choose videos” target
  • Clip tiles: thumbnail, duration, star (“must include”), remove
  • “Similar” badge on near-duplicate clips
  • Footer: “6 clips · 0:43 of 10:00 max” + Continue

States

  • Empty
  • Importing (per-tile spinner)
  • Ready, one starred
  • Tile error: “This browser can’t decode … video”
  • Limits hit: toast (30 clips, 10 min, 4 GB)

Limits & formats

  • MP4, MOV, WebM
  • Up to 30 clips, 10 minutes, 4 GB
  • Vertical or horizontal; horizontal clips get cropped to vertical automatically
/p/:id/script · step 2

Script

The story, as short first-person lines, one per moment. The user writes them, or taps “Write it for me” and AI writes them from what’s in the clips. Each line becomes a scene and is read aloud.

Content

  • Title (shown big at the start of the video)
  • “What’s it about?” optional prompt
  • Line list: drag to reorder, remove, Enter adds a line
  • Write it for me / Rewrite with AI · Polish my lines
  • “About 0:13 spoken” estimate
  • Settings chips: Format, Length, Vibe
  • Footer: No script · Continue

States

  • Empty (one blank line with an example placeholder)
  • AI writing (buttons busy)
  • Filled
  • “Replace your lines?” confirm before rewriting

Settings values

  • Format: Vertical 9:16 (default), Square 1:1, Wide 16:9
  • Length: Auto (default), 15s, 30s, 60s
  • Vibe: Auto, Chill, Upbeat, Cinematic, Funny, Aesthetic
/p/:id/voice · step 3

Voice

Who tells the story: an AI voice, the user’s own voice, or nobody (lines appear as captions only).

AI voice

  • Six voices: Ava, Leo, Mia, Sam (American), Grace, Oliver (British)
  • Each has a one-line description and a play button that reads the user’s first line

Record my own

  • List of lines with Record / Redo and play
  • Teleprompter: one line at a time, big text, a large record button, a live level meter, timer, Previous / Skip
  • Auto-advances after each take
  • Footer counts “2 of 7 lines recorded”; Continue waits until all are done

States

  • AI selected, preview loading / playing
  • Record list, partly recorded
  • Teleprompter idle / recording / saving
  • Microphone blocked (toast)
  • No voiceover
/p/:id/generate · step 4

Make

The AI builds the vlog. Usually under a minute. This is the one waiting moment in the product, so it deserves care: honest stages, real progress, and something pleasant to look at.

Stages (in order)

  • Watching your clips (shows “3 of 6 clips”)
  • Voicing your script (“2 of 7 lines”)
  • Picking the best moments
  • Cutting it together
  • Adding captions & music

States

  • In progress
  • Error: the failed stage is marked, with a plain message, Try again and Go back
  • On success it moves straight to Edit

Idea space

  • Clip thumbnails or the title animating while it works
  • A sign-in prompt can appear here when a guest runs out of free vlogs
/p/:id/edit · step 5

Edit

The vlog plays right away. Below it are the scenes (one per script line) and three panels for the whole video. At the bottom, users can type a change for the AI (“open with the sunset”). Undo and redo walk through versions.

Always visible

  • Undo / redo, note about the current version, Export
  • Phone-shaped preview with play / scrub / time
  • Tabs: Scenes, Voice, Music, Style
  • “Tell the AI” input with suggestion chips

Scene sheet (tap a scene)

  • Edit the narration line (re-voices just that line)
  • Each shot: thumbnail, range, clip-sound toggle (off / quiet / full)
  • Swap: grid of other good moments
  • Trim: start / end sliders
  • Move earlier / later, Delete scene

Panels

  • Voice: switch AI voice, voice volume
  • Music: five tracks + No music, volume (music dips under speech automatically)
  • Style: title text, caption style presets
Known weak spots to solve
  • On a phone the sticky “Tell the AI” bar and the tabs compete for space. Consider collapsing Tell the AI into a button that opens a sheet.
  • The scene sheet covers the preview; users can’t see the change they’re making. A shorter sheet or a mini preview inside it would help.
  • Scene cards show the first shot only; a scene can have up to three shots.
Export sheet (from Edit)

Export & share

The video is rendered on the phone itself. Then the native share sheet opens (save to Photos, TikTok, Instagram…) or, on desktop, it downloads.

States

  • Ready: size, length, “made on this device, nothing is uploaded”
  • Rendering: progress %, “Keep this tab open”, Cancel
  • Done: playable result, file size, Share, Save
  • Error: message + Try again

Facts

  • 1080×1920 for vertical, 30 fps, MP4 (WebM on some browsers)
  • A 30 s vlog takes roughly 20–40 s to render
Sign-in sheet (any screen)

Sign in

Guests get one free vlog. After that, or when a daily limit is hit, this sheet explains why and offers an email sign-in link (Google sign-in can be switched on).

States

  • Enter email
  • Sending
  • “Check your email for a sign-in link”
  • Error
  • Signed in: email + sign out on Home
The video itself

Titles and captions are part of your design

Every exported vlog carries a big title for the first three seconds and captions of the narration, a few words at a time, with the word being spoken highlighted. These are drawn into the video pixels, so they become what users post. Please design them as a small set of presets with exact specs.

TikTok, Reels and Shorts cover the top bar, the bottom fifth and a column on the right with their own buttons. Today the title is centred at 15% of the frame height and the captions at 70%, both within 84% of the width. Keep your presets inside those zones.

Rules & constraints

Things the build needs from the design

  • Fonts must be web fonts we can bundle (Google Fonts or a licence that allows web and video embedding). Caption fonts are drawn into exported videos, so check the licence covers that.
  • Touch targets at least 44×44 px. Primary actions sit in the bottom footer, within thumb reach.
  • Respect the phone’s safe areas (notch, home bar). The header and footer already pad for them.
  • Contrast: WCAG AA for text in both themes. Focus states must be visible for keyboard users.
  • Motion: keep it short and purposeful; provide a reduced-motion fallback for anything that moves.
  • Icons: the build uses Lucide line icons. Another set is fine if it covers: play, pause, mic, star, scissors, swap, music, palette, film, send, undo, redo, share, download.
  • No timeline UI, by product decision.
  • Out of scope for now: marketing site, pricing pages, photos as input, templates, multiple languages, collaboration. Leave room for a future “Templates” entry point on Script and an “Upgrade” spot on Home and Export.
